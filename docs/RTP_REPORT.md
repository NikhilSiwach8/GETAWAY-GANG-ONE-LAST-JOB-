## 2. Independent Monte Carlo of the reel model

`math/tools/stratified_sim.py` runs the real SDK `GameState.run_spin` with seeds that do not overlap the book generation. RTP is decomposed exactly by the initial-board Scatter count:

`RTP = P(<3 S)·E[win | base] + P(3 S)·E[win | Heist] + P(4+ S)·E[win | Escape]`

The trigger probabilities come from the reel strips analytically. The conditional means are measured.
